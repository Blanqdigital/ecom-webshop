# Product page structure

The sample storefront is a wiring demo, not a finished product-page design.
Every new store needs the visual structure below before storefront review.
A product image followed by text sections is not enough. This checklist
complements LANDING-PAGE.md; evidence requirements override its example claims.

Structural reference: https://www.veluni.shop/ (reviewed 2026-09-10).
Use its hierarchy and pacing, not its product, brand, offers or promises.
This is a design reference, not evidence of conversion performance.

## Purchase section

1. Large gallery beside a compact purchase panel on desktop, stacked on mobile.
   Distinct image roles: actual product, use context, material/form detail.
   Recolours are variants, not a substitute for a gallery. Add thumbnails,
   arrows, image count and accessible enlargement. Test keyboard and touch.
2. Product name and unit price, followed by three short, supported benefits.
   An oversized slogan must not displace the product's identity and choices.
3. Named visual variant tiles. Selected state and product photo must agree.
   Colour cannot be the only selected-state indicator.
4. Explicit quantity/package choices where useful, with contents and total.
   No invented savings, free items or popularity badges. Prices must agree
   with server pricing, cart and fulfilment quantities.
5. Prominent add-to-cart button showing the selected total, then concise
   verified reassurance or an honest prelaunch state.
6. Product details and delivery/returns accordions directly below the buy box.

## Below the purchase section

1. Narrow three-item benefit strip with restrained icons.
2. Lifestyle section: one short idea, heading, short paragraph, product link
   and a large photo or asymmetric photo pair.
3. Visual three-step use sequence based on actual product instructions.
4. Material/form split: close product view beside a few concrete details.
5. Practical FAQ in accordions, not an expanded wall of copy.
6. Clear closing product CTA and complete seller/policy footer.

Alternate image-led sections and compact information. Do not fill missing
evidence slots with generated before/after results, fake reviews, authority
badges or unsupported health claims. Identify AI editorial imagery as
illustration and document it; do not use it as construction/specification proof.
Use actual supplier/owned photos for variants.

## Review gate

- Inspect desktop and 390px mobile: top, controls, middle and end.
- Check overflow, loaded images, readable labels and tap targets.
- Exercise gallery, enlargement, Escape, variant, quantity, total and cart.
- Verify accordions and return-to-product links.
- Repeat essential checks on the completed production deployment.
- Record missing physical photography or unverified instructions as launch work.

Completed layout is not demonstrated conversion optimization. Measure actual
add-to-cart and checkout completion after launch before claiming improvement.
